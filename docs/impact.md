# Impact — AI Sustainability Discovery

## Expected Impact

### Immediate (Prototype Stage)
- **Structured reporting**: Converts informal sustainability observations into structured, persistent, searchable records
- **AI-assisted prioritization**: Reduces cognitive load on reviewers by providing an initial priority assessment
- **Accountability through tracking**: Every status change is logged — creating a verifiable audit trail
- **Dashboard visibility**: Real-time aggregate view of issue status distribution

### Anticipated (With Community Adoption)
- Reduction in mean time from problem identification to action being planned
- Pattern identification across categories and locations enables proactive management
- Community ownership and engagement in sustainability outcomes
- Evidence base for sustainability planning and resource allocation

## Honest Impact Metrics

> **Important**: This platform is a prototype. The impact metrics shown are based on report lifecycle data, not on-site environmental measurements.

| Metric | What It Means | What We Claim |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Reports Submitted | Community members engaged | Actual count from database |
| Reports Resolved | Issues addressed by a human | Actual count from database |
| Reports Verified | Resolutions confirmed | Actual count from database |
| Resolution Rate | % of reports resolved | Calculated from real data |
| Reports with Photo Evidence | Evidence quality | Actual count from database |

### What We Do NOT Claim
- CO₂ emissions saved (no measurement data)
- Litres of water conserved (no measurement data)
- Kilowatt-hours of energy saved (no measurement data)
- Economic value of interventions (no economic data)

These may become measurable with integration to IoT sensors or municipal data systems — documented as future scope.
