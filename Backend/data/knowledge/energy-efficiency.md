# Energy Efficiency & Clean Power Utilization

## Problem Identification & Impact
Excessive electrical energy consumption in educational, commercial, and residential facilities drives up greenhouse gas emissions and operational costs. Unattended HVAC systems, inefficient lighting, and old appliances represent major energy sinks.

## Root Causes
- Manual control of lighting and air conditioning in unoccupied halls or classrooms.
- Deployment of outdated fluorescent lamps (T8/T12) instead of energy-efficient LEDs.
- Lack of sub-metering to isolate high-consumption zones or equipment faults.
- Continuous standby power draw (phantom loads) from idle electronic equipment.

## Actionable Mitigation Strategies
1. **Lighting Upgrades:** Replace legacy fluorescent bulbs with high-efficiency LED lights integrated with occupancy sensors.
2. **Smart HVAC & Controls:** Program programmable thermostats, clean air filters monthly, and install motion sensors in common spaces.
3. **Renewable Integration:** Install rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) panels to offset peak electricity demand.
4. **Behavioral Interventions:** Display clear energy conservation prompts near light switches and implement automated scheduled shut-offs for non-essential loads.
