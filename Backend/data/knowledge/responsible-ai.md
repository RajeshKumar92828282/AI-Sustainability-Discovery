# Responsible AI & Governance Guidelines

## Core Principles in AI for Sustainability
Artificial Intelligence systems used in environmental discovery and decision-making must adhere to strict ethical and governance principles to ensure trust, safety, and accountability.

## Key Principles
1. **Assistive Decision Support:** AI outputs are advisory recommendations, not absolute truth or automated policy decisions. Human oversight is mandatory.
2. **Transparency & Grounding:** AI analysis must disclose supporting sources (via Retrieval-Augmented Generation) so users can verify the evidence behind recommendations.
3. **Data Privacy:** User-submitted reports must exclude personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal phone numbers, names, or private addresses.
4. **Low-Confidence Flags:** When retrieval scores or AI confidence fall below designated thresholds, the system must explicitly display a warning recommending human review.
5. **Separation of Facts vs Context:** AI models must not hallucinate government regulations or external policies unless backed by retrieved knowledge base documents.

## Operational Protocol
- **Human Validation:** All recommended operational actions (e.g. dispatching maintenance crews or altering infrastructure) require verification by authorized human managers.
- **Continuous Monitoring:** AI outputs should be regularly audited for bias, drift, or inaccurate categorizations.
