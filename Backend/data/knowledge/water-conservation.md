# Water Conservation & Drainage Infrastructure

## Problem Identification & Impact
Water leakages, overflowing storage tanks, broken plumbing fixtures, and inefficient irrigation cause significant loss of potable water resources. Accumulation of stagnant water also creates breeding grounds for mosquitoes and damages building foundations.

## Root Causes
- Aging plumbing pipes and worn-out flush mechanisms in restrooms.
- Missing automatic shut-off valves on overhead storage tanks.
- Poorly maintained drainage systems prone to clogging by debris and leaves.
- Over-watering of gardens without moisture sensors or drip irrigation.

## Actionable Mitigation Strategies
1. **Infrastructure Repair:** Conduct urgent plumbing audits to fix leaking taps, broken pipes, and continuously running flush valves.
2. **Automated Controls:** Install float switches and automated solenoid valves on water tanks to prevent overflow.
3. **Rainwater Harvesting:** Retrofit rooftop rainwater catchment systems to recharge groundwater aquifers and supply non-potable uses.
4. **Conservation Tech:** Upgrade restrooms with low-flow aerators and sensor-activated faucets to cut consumption by 30-50%.
