# Solid Waste Management & Circular Economy

## Problem Identification & Impact
Unmanaged solid waste accumulation near entrances, cafeterias, and public areas causes severe environmental and health risks. Overflowing waste bins lead to disease vector proliferation, offensive odors, surface water contamination, and plastic pollution.

## Root Causes
- Irregular waste collection schedules relative to usage volume.
- Absence of source-segregation bins (separating wet organic waste from dry recyclables).
- Lack of public awareness and proper disposal signage.
- Over-reliance on single-use plastics in food service facilities.

## Actionable Mitigation Strategies
1. **Immediate Operations:** Increase bin emptying frequency during peak operational hours and install high-capacity, covered waste receptacles.
2. **Infrastructure:** Install color-coded 3-bin segregation systems (Green for Biodegradable, Blue for Recyclable, Red for Hazardous/Rejected).
3. **Resource Recovery:** Establish localized composting units for cafeteria organic waste to divert waste from landfills.
4. **Policy & Engagement:** Mandate reusable cutlery in campus canteens and conduct student/staff awareness drives.

## SDG Alignment
- **SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption & Production):** Target 12.5 (Substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling, and reuse).
- **SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities):** Target 11.6 (Reduce municipal environmental impact).
