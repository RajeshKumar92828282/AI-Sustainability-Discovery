# Sustainable Cities and Communities (SDG 11)

## Overview & Scope
Sustainable Development Goal 11 aims to make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable. Rapid urbanization creates environmental challenges including waste accumulation, traffic congestion, energy inefficiency, and degraded public sanitation.

## Key Targets & Focus Areas
- **Target 11.6:** Reduce the adverse per capita environmental impact of cities, focusing on air quality and municipal waste management.
- **Target 11.7:** Provide universal access to safe, inclusive, and accessible green and public spaces.
- **Target 11.3:** Enhance inclusive and sustainable urbanization and capacity for participatory urban planning.

## Common Urban Sustainability Issues
- Uncollected municipal solid waste in public spaces and educational institutions.
- Overcrowded public transit systems leading to excessive private vehicle usage.
- Inadequate stormwater drainage resulting in waterlogging and localized flooding.
- Lack of energy monitoring in public buildings and campus facilities.

## Recommended Community & Municipal Actions
1. Deploy segregated waste bins (organic, recyclable, hazardous) across high-footfall campus and urban areas.
2. Establish community-led environmental reporting tools to track infrastructure breakdowns.
3. Promote walkable campus pathways and micro-mobility infrastructure (bicycles, electric shuttles).
4. Implement smart energy controls and LED lighting retrofits for institutional facilities.
