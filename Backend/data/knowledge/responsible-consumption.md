# Responsible Consumption & Production (SDG 12)

## Problem Identification & Impact
Linear "take-make-dispose" patterns result in resource depletion, excessive electronic waste (e-waste), plastic accumulation, and greenhouse gas emissions from material production and disposal.

## Key Principles of Circular Economy
- **Refuse & Reduce:** Minimize single-use items, over-packaging, and unnecessary resource extraction.
- **Reuse & Repair:** Extend product lifespans through maintenance, repair cafes, and equipment sharing.
- **Recycle & Recover:** Process waste streams into secondary raw materials for new products.

## Practical Actions for Communities & Institutions
1. **Procurement Policy:** Enforce sustainable purchasing guidelines prioritizing recycled materials, local suppliers, and energy-efficient appliances.
2. **E-Waste Management:** Set up dedicated collection hubs for discarded electronics, batteries, and cables to ensure safe recycling.
3. **Food Waste Minimization:** Monitor cafeteria food surplus, partner with food redistribution charities, and convert food scraps into bio-gas or compost.
4. **Paperless Workflows:** Transition institutional administration to digital-first documentation to reduce paper consumption.

## SDG Alignment
- **SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production):** Target 12.2 (Sustainable management of natural resources) & Target 12.5 (Waste reduction).
